STD testing, without the U.S. price tag

Confidential screening at a fraction of what the same panels cost in El Paso. Read how it works before you cross.

How it works

Two ways to get tested

Pick whichever suits you. In both, the sample is taken in the morning, fasting.

Samples are not taken at the doctor's office

Blood draws and swabs are done at one of the three collection sites listed below, in the morning, after 8 hours of fasting. The office (Monday to Friday, 3 to 5 PM) is for your consultation and for going over your results.

They are two separate visits at two different times of day. If you cross the bridge at 3 PM expecting blood work in the office, it cannot be done there.

Route 1

Consultation first

Best if you have symptoms, questions, or you are not sure which tests you need.

  1. See me at the office, Monday to Friday, 3 to 5 PM. We decide which tests you need and I hand you the lab order.
  2. On another day, in the morning, go to the collection site fasting for 8 hours.
  3. The lab emails your results directly to you.
  4. Come back to the office so I can explain what they mean and start treatment if you need it.
Route 2

Lab order by WhatsApp

Best if you already know which test you want and would rather skip a first visit.

  1. Message me on WhatsApp and I send you the lab order digitally. You do not need to come in for this.
  2. Go in the morning, fasting for 8 hours, to the collection site. No appointment needed.
  3. The lab emails your results directly to you.
  4. Bring the results to the office so we can interpret them together and decide the next step.
